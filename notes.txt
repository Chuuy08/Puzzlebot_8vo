Lanzar micro_ros_agent

1. Visualizar en rviz 
ros2 launch puzzlebot_description robot_state_publisher_launch.py

2. Simulacion gazebo - 1 carro
ros2 launch puzzlebot_gazebo bringup_simulation_simple_launch.py

3. Simulacion gazebo - 2 carros
ros2 launch puzzlebot_gazebo bringup_simulation_multi_launch.py

4. Simulacion gazebo con todos los parametros explicitos
ros2 launch puzzlebot_gazebo gazebo_example_launch.py

5. Control real del puzzlebot_description
ros2 launch puzzlebot_control puzzlebot_launch.py

6. Control de dos robots reales simultáneos:
ros2 launch puzzlebot_control multi_puzzlebot_launch.py

7. Lanzar camera
ros2 launch ros_deep_learning video_source.ros2.launch \
  input_width:=320 input_height:=180



8. Dashboard web puzzlebot_gui (3 terminales)

  Terminal 1 — Simulador (localización + navegación):
  ros2 launch puzzlebot_localisation mcl_launch.py
  ros2 launch puzzlebot_navigation navigation_launch.py

  Terminal 2 — Servidor web + Bridge ROS-gRPC:
  source ~/robotics_ws/install/setup.bash
  cd ~/robotics_ws/install/puzzlebot_gui/share/puzzlebot_gui/web
  python3 -m http.server 8080 &
  ros2 run puzzlebot_gui gui_bridge

  Terminal 3 — Proxy gRPC-Web:
  cd ~/robotics_ws/install/puzzlebot_gui/share/puzzlebot_gui
  ./grpcwebproxy --backend_addr=localhost:50051 --run_tls_server=false --allow_all_origins --server_http_debug_port=8443

  Abrir en navegador: http://localhost:8080

  Para robot real cambiar mcl_launch.py -> mcl_real_launch.py
                      y navigation_launch.py -> navigation_real_launch.py

  Nota: la ruta en el mapa aparece al enviar el primer waypoint desde la pagina.
